Your First Adventure Starts Here

Create a character, help the villagers and learn your first skill as you take your first steps.

BackMir Guide · v1.4.7 · Updated 2026-09-19

Before You Enter

Choose Start Adventure to create a character; no account is needed. Progress stays in this browser. Changing devices or browsers does not transfer your character, and clearing site data deletes local saves. Use a normal window in your usual browser.

Step 1 · Choose Your Class

Warriors fight up close, Wizards cast ranged spells, and Taoists combine summons with support. A Warrior is a straightforward way to learn the controls. All three classes receive their first skill through the opening quests.

Step 2 · Find the Novice Guide

Speak to the Novice Guide and accept First Steps. Defeat 5 Hens, return to turn it in, then follow Meet the Butcher and Provisions for the Road. Turning in quests unlocks the next steps, so do more than hunt monsters.

Step 3 · Use your skill and gather supplies

Turn in First Steps after defeating 5 Hens to receive your first class skill book and potions. Open your bag and double-click the book to learn.

Step 4 · Equip Yourself and Stock Up

New characters start with a Wooden Sword and cloth armor equipped, and a health potion bound to 1. Maomao’s Blessing is placed in your bag automatically: press B and double-click it to equip. Add mana potions to the second hotbar slot when available. Check supplies and bag space before leaving.

Step 5 · Take the station route to the tomb

The Next step panel follows the early quest chain through the chicken hunt, Butcher visit, venison, Blacksmith trial, board delivery, Longyang Grass and Scorpion Tails. Turning in Longyang Grass rewards one Town Scroll. For the tail quest, use the village or Bichon station to teleport near the Orc Tomb entrance, enter Floor 1 and pick up a scorpion tail. Use the scroll to return to your home town, then visit the Peddler. The village Peddler sells more Town Scrolls. Continue with finding Xiaobao and reporting back to the Blacksmith before exploring the city.

What Next?

Try the next map when you can comfortably defeat the monsters where you are. Improve your skills, supplies and equipment before challenging bosses. Suggested map levels are guidance, not a guarantee that your current loadout will succeed.
