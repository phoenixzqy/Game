Start in three steps

  1. Pick a gamePress Play on a game. The first visit loads the game files.
  2. Create a characterChoose Warrior, Wizard or Taoist and follow the starter quests to learn moving, fighting and your bag.
  3. Your progress savesCharacters save automatically in this browser. In games with cloud saves, signing in syncs that game to its own cloud save.

Playing on PC, Mac or phone

  • PC and Mac

    Open your browser; there is no client to install. Hold the left mouse button to move, the right one to run, and click an enemy to attack. F picks up, F1–F8 cast skills, B opens your bag, C your character and M the map.

  • Phone

    Play in landscape with the on-screen touch controls. The first start loads the game files, so Wi-Fi helps.

  • Before you switch devices

    Each game’s save guide explains saving, syncing and moving to another device. Read it before switching devices or clearing browser data.

About BackMir

BackMir (旧境传说) is a collection of browser Mir-style RPGs at backmir.org, available in Chinese, English and Spanish. Every game is free to play in PC and mobile browsers. Characters and saves are kept per game, and the guides are written for each game separately.

Questions

What is BackMir?

BackMir (Chinese name 旧境传说) is a collection of Mir-style role-playing games that run in the browser. It has two games: BackMir Classic, a classic three-class loot hunt, and Darkforged, built around random affixes, legendaries and runewords. Classic also offers Hell: the same world and core gameplay with slower progression, rarer loot and separate characters and saves.

Is it free? Do I need to download anything?

It is free to play and there is nothing to download or install. The first time you start a game, it loads its files over the internet.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. On a phone, play in landscape with the on-screen touch controls. On a computer, use the keyboard and mouse. Macs work in the browser too.

Do the games share saves?

No. Each game keeps its own characters, bags and progress, and never imports or overwrites another game’s save. Your account is shared; each game with cloud saves syncs only its own. Each game’s save guide says what it currently supports.

How are Classic and Darkforged different?

Both have the Warrior, Wizard and Taoist and the same world. Classic is about map routes, target items and steady progression. Darkforged replaces the item system with random affixes, 40 legendaries, 13 sets and runewords, so comparing drops and building matter more.

Will there be more games?

Yes. New Mir-style games with different rules will be added over time, each with its own page, guides and separate saves.
