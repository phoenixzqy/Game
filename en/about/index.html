Adventurer’s Handbook · v1.4.7

Make Every Adventure Count

The official BackMir site: play without an account and explore the game guide.

Available now

Play without signing up and save your progress in this browser. Browse guides, quests, maps, NPCs, skills and recipes.

Cloud uploads across devices

Sign in to the same game account to sync at character selection across all three languages. Wait for “Synced” before changing devices. Checkpoint times do not decide which progress wins. The cloud keeps one current save plus up to two private recovery copies before a manual choice, full load or restore. Recovery copies expire after seven days. Local saving works offline.

Account and saves

Google sign-in supports the community and cloud saves. Characters sync at selection; conflicting progress requires a choice. Automatic sync can be disabled. Paid memberships are not available.

Local saves

Characters are saved in this browser. Guest saves have no cloud backup: clearing site data may erase your progress. Saves do not automatically transfer between devices, browsers or website addresses.

Site analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count page visits and play-button clicks so we can improve the site. Analytics uses cookies and browser visit data. We do not send character names, save data or search queries, and advertising personalization is disabled.

Google Privacy Policy · Google Analytics Opt-out

How the guide is maintained

Guide articles are reviewed for the current release, and reference pages are generated from the same game data. This guide describes the current browser edition; requirements and messages shown in the game still apply.
