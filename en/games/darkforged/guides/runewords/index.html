Darkforged Runewords: Recipes, Bases & Rune Order

These are Darkforged recipes, using Rune 1 through Rune 8; Diablo II and Diablo IV recipes do not apply here. Choose a normal base of the listed type with exactly the required number of sockets, then insert the runes in the recipe’s order. Magic, rare, legendary and set bases cannot form runewords.

Updated 2026-11-05

Check the base before spending runes

An open socket is only the first check. A two-rune recipe needs exactly two sockets; placing two runes into a three-socket item does not form that two-rune word. Weapons, body armour and helmets are not interchangeable bases.

Order, effects and required level

A completed word adds its extra effects alongside the runes’ own properties. The level below is the minimum contributed by its runes, not a promise that any base can be equipped at that level. A native base or another requirement can be higher; this item’s requirement reduction is applied afterwards.

Preview in the workshop

Read missing requirements in the in-game recipe book, then use the socketing page to choose the rune and target. Inserting a rune spends it. Check the selected item, existing runes and order before confirming; an incorrectly filled base is not an empty base.
