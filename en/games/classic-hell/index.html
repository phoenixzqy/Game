Overview

FAQ

Higher difficulty · Casual players, choose carefully

Hell has slower progression and harder-to-find equipment. There are no fireworks, no extra Classic drop-rate boosts, and many Classic experience and convenience bonuses are removed. It is intended for players who enjoy long-term progression and a tougher challenge. For a more relaxed experience and faster character growth, choose Classic.

Can characters share storage?

An 80-slot shared stash is available at warehouse NPCs for characters in one Hell save. Equipment and skill books only; bound items and starter gifts are excluded. Gold blocks, gold bars and other items cannot be stored. Cloud conflicts select one complete save, including all characters and shared storage.

How do gold blocks drop?

Eligible Bosses independently have a 2% chance to drop one 200,000-gold block, unaffected by item-drop bonuses.

Are there fireworks?

No fireworks or fireworks bonuses.

Can I transfer a Classic character?

No. Start a new character; saves stay separate.
