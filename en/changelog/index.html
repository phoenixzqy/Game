2026-11-25

1.4.27 / 2.0.23 / 1.0.17 · Mobile shortcut bar update

  • Tap the gear beside the item bar to set items and skills. It remembers the last tab you used.
  • The item bar shows all six slots when space allows. On smaller screens, swipe in the direction of the arrow and tap a slot to use it.
  • Pick up loot just above and inward from the main attack button. Switch targets at the outer edge. The layout mirrors for left-handed play.

2026-11-25

1.4.26 / 2.0.22 / 1.0.16 · Pet growth, Warrior buffs and Darkforged material stacks

  • Across all three games, Blood Dragon and Diamond Body last five minutes per cast at skill rank 3 and above.
  • Tamed Wizard pets gain health, attack, physical defence and magic defence as they level up. Their name colour reflects their level.
  • Monster names and health bars appear in front of terrain. A successful freeze from Frost Crunch or Ice Flame adds an F beside the target’s name.
  • Classic: the ordinary White Tiger’s base attack range has increased from 10–10 to 12–16.
  • Darkforged: salvaged essences and crafting materials stack to 999. Use Sort in the materials bag to combine existing stacks.
  • Darkforged Taoists who know Soul Fireball can enable auto-casting in Assist. They prioritise the locked enemy in range and their pets focus it. Fire skills deal 50% extra damage when the ring successfully freezes an enemy.

2026-11-24

1.4.25 / 2.0.21 / 1.0.15 · Quick swap for the Teleport Ring

  • Desktop: right-click an empty item shortcut, pick the Teleport Ring from your bag, then choose the left or right ring to replace.
  • Mobile: open settings beside the item shortcuts. Pick a slot, select your Teleport Ring, then choose the hand wearing the ring you want to swap.
  • Use the shortcut once to equip the Teleport Ring and again to restore the original ring. On desktop, keys 1–6 also work. You can swap with a full bag.

  • Tamed foxes keep their native attack range while their signature skill recharges.
  • Dungeon and Mourning Moon fox quest descriptions show the kill count for your edition.

2026-11-22

1.4.22 / 2.0.18 / 1.0.12 · Crafting, gear exchanges and late-game challenges

  • Classic 1.4.22, Darkforged 2.0.18 and Dream Hell 1.0.12.
  • Ore, wood, gem and cloth processing reach level 4 faster in all three editions. Trial floor 52 shows the boss's remaining revives, and Secret Realm Passes and Bagua Talismans are protected from shop sales.

Crafting progression from levels 1 to 6 now reflects the materials used across all five skills. In Dream Hell, each Yanmo Prince kill awards 1–3 Fire Dragon Hearts, even if you already carry one.
  • Save your progress and update from Save & System. Already downloaded offline assets can be reused.

2026-11-22

Dream Hell 1.0.11 · Dream Hell crafting, late-game loot and challenges

  • The Infernal Prince can drop the Heart of the Fire Dragon. Pumpkin Seeds drop again. Each of the first three Dragonbreaker challenge clears awards one medal for your class.
