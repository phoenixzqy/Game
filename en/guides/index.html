BackMir Classic

Live v1.4.12

A classic Mir-style adventure: pick a Warrior, Wizard or Taoist, leave Bichon on your first quest and work your way through maps and bosses towards the next item you want.

  • Getting startedCreate a character, finish the first quests, learn a skill.
  • Classes and skillsWhat each class does and when it learns its skills.
  • Maps and routesExits, monsters and where the bosses are.
  • Equipment basicsStars, bonus points, affixes and requirements.
  • Drop finderLook up which monsters drop an item.
  • Forge and cubeMaterials, costs and recipes.
  • Saves and changing devicesClassic characters save in this browser and upload to the Classic cloud save after you sign in. Characters, bags and progress are never shared with another game.

BackMir Hell

New game v1.0.3

Hell has slower progression and harder-to-find equipment. There are no fireworks, no extra Classic drop-rate boosts, and many Classic experience and convenience bonuses are removed. It is intended for players who enjoy long-term progression and a tougher challenge.

  • Hell rulesExperience, loot, exceptional items, Boss gold and retained features.
  • Saves and changing devicesShared site account, separate characters, equipment, cloud saves and rankings.

Darkforged

New game v2.0.8

A free single-player action RPG in your browser. Choose a Warrior, Wizard or Taoist, find your next upgrade and turn the right socketed base into a runeword. No client installation needed.

  • Getting started: quests and your first upgradeQuest tracking, first skill, supplies and your first item swap.
  • Equipment: affixes, levels and favouritesItem level versus required level; filters and comparisons.
  • Runewords: bases, sockets and orderEvery runeword with its base, sockets, order and effects.
  • The workshop: salvage, enchant and cubeEssences, one-affix enchanting, socketing and cube recipes.
  • Saves and changing devicesDarkforged characters, bags and progress belong to Darkforged only. Its browser and cloud saves are separate from Classic and cannot be imported, merged or overwritten. Your site sign-in works for both.
